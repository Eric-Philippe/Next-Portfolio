## Project Overview

This phone stand was born from a need for a clean, unobtrusive way to keep my phone visible during work hours. The design prioritizes minimal footprint while maintaining stability.

## Design Process

Started with sketches in Fusion 360, iterating on the angle multiple times to find the perfect viewing position for video calls. The final design uses a 65-degree angle which works great for both portrait and landscape orientations.

## Print Settings

- Layer Height: 0.2mm
- Infill: 20% Gyroid
- Supports: None needed
- Print Speed: 150mm/s

## Result

The matte black finish complements my desk setup perfectly. The print came out clean with no visible layer lines thanks to the X1 Carbon's precision.
