## The Problem

Cables everywhere. USB-C, lightning, audio cables - they all end up in a tangled mess on the desk.

## The Solution

A modular clip system that can be connected together to route cables cleanly along the edge of the desk. Each module holds 2-3 cables securely.

## Design Features

- Snap-fit connectors for modularity
- Rounded edges to prevent cable damage
- Adhesive mounting option on the bottom
- Stackable design

## Print Details

Printed in PETG for flexibility and durability. The snap-fit joints need the slight give that PETG provides.

Used 0.16mm layer height for smoother surfaces where the cables rest.
