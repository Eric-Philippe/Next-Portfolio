## Product Vision

Cril Bot was created to meet the needs of the Languages and International Relations Centre at Université Paul Sabatier: automate student assistance, simplify administration, and provide a modern experience on Discord.

### Why Cril Bot?

The goal was to build a flexible, secure, and scalable tool, fully integrated with Discord and Google Sheets. TypeScript brings enhanced reliability and easier maintenance.

### Key Features

> All features are documented in a comprehensive GitBook, accessible [here](https://eric-p.gitbook.io/cril-bot-documentation).

- **Interactive Assistance**  
  A dedicated Discord channel for student help, with automated responses and an intuitive interface.

- **Google Sheets Integration**  
  Two-way sync with Google Sheets for managing lists, registrations, and tracking.

- **Complete Logging System**  
  All actions and interactions are logged in PostgreSQL for precise and secure tracking.

- **Slash Commands & Plugins**  
  Supports slash commands, context menus, and plugins (polls, toss, calculator, etc.).

- **Security & Reliability**  
  TypeScript ensures data safety and error prevention, crucial for student management.

- **Full GitBook Documentation**  
  Comprehensive and accessible documentation for users and admins.

### Use Cases

- **Student registration and list management**
- **Automated responses and assistance**
- **Tracking and reporting via logs**
- **Seamless integration with Discord and Google Sheets**

## Architecture Overview

Cril Bot combines Discord.js, TypeScript, and PostgreSQL for a robust, scalable, and secure architecture. Google Sheets integration enables smooth administrative data management.

### Main Technologies

- **TypeScript**: Code safety and maintainability.
- **Discord.js**: Advanced Discord integration.
- **PostgreSQL**: Reliable log and data storage.
- **Google Sheets API**: List and tracking sync.
- **Docker**: Simplified deployment.

### Features

- **Automated assistance**: Dedicated channel and interactive commands.
- **Complete logs**: Precise tracking of actions and events.
- **Plugins & commands**: Extensions for polls, toss, calculations, etc.
- **GitBook documentation**: Full guide for users and admins.

## Technical Details

### Architecture

- **Discord Bot**: Handles interactions, commands, and assistance.
- **TypeScript Backend**: Secure and structured codebase.
- **PostgreSQL Database**: Stores logs and critical data.
- **Google Sheets**: Admin interface for management.

### Security

- **TypeScript**: Strong typing to prevent errors.
- **Logs**: Full traceability of actions.
- **Discord Permissions**: Fine-grained access control.
