## Product Vision

Glitch & Books was created to solve a simple problem: keeping track of your book collection, wherever you are. Whether you want to lend a book, check your reading stats, or just browse your library, Glitch & Books offers a secure, dynamic, and user-friendly solution.

### Why Glitch & Books?

Most book management apps are either too complex, too limited, or locked behind subscriptions. Glitch & Books is free, open, and designed for everyone. Its responsive interface adapts to any device, making your library accessible at home or on the go.

### Main Features

- **Add & Edit Books**  
  Easily add new books, edit details, and keep your collection up to date.

- **Dynamic Library View**  
  Browse your library with a modern interface, optimized for desktop and mobile.

- **Sort & Filter**  
  Instantly sort and filter by title, author, date, genre, and more.

- **Secure Access**  
  Your data is protected on an HTTPS server.

- **Stats & Fun Comparisons**  
  See graphs and quirky stats about your reading habits.

- **Export & Share**  
  Export your library as PDF, Excel, JSON, XML, or CSV. Share with friends.

- **Random Book & Suggestions**  
  Discover new reads with a random book button and suggestions carousel.

### Use Cases

- **Personal Library Management**: Track your collection, reading progress, and wishlists.
- **Lending & Borrowing**: Know exactly which books you have and who borrowed them.
- **Stats & Insights**: Visualize your reading habits and compare with others.
- **Sharing & Exporting**: Share your library or export it for backup.

## Architecture Overview

Glitch & Books is built for flexibility and security, using a modern tech stack and responsive design.

### Core Technologies

- **Node.js & Express**: Fast, scalable backend with TypeScript.
- **React**: Dynamic, responsive front-end.
- **Postgres & TypeORM**: Reliable database and ORM for data management.
- **Docker**: Easy deployment and scalability.

### Features

- **Dynamic Library**: Add, edit, delete, sort, and filter books.
- **Responsive UI**: Optimized for desktop and mobile.
- **Secure HTTPS**: Protects your data and privacy.
- **Stats & Graphs**: Fun insights into your reading.
- **Export & Share**: Multiple formats for exporting your library.

## Technical Details

### Responsive Interface

- **Desktop & Mobile Views**: Automatically adapts for optimal experience.
- **Fast Sorting & Filtering**: Real-time updates as you search or sort.

### Security

- **HTTPS**: All data is encrypted.
- **User Authentication**: Basic user system, with plans for advanced accounts.

### Export Options

- **PDF, Excel, JSON, XML, CSV**: Choose your preferred format for backup or sharing.
